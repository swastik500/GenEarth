# Pollution Control and Air Quality Management in India

## Understanding India's Pollution Challenge

India is home to **22 of the world's 30 most polluted cities**. Air pollution causes an estimated **1.67 million premature deaths** annually in India. The problem affects everyone, but understanding and taking action can make a difference.

## Types of Pollution

### 1. Air Pollution

**Major Sources:**

- Vehicle emissions (30-40% in urban areas)
- Industrial emissions (20-25%)
- Construction dust (15-20%)
- Biomass burning and crop residue (15-20% seasonal)
- Waste burning

**Common Pollutants:**

- **PM2.5 & PM10**: Fine particulate matter (most dangerous)
- **NO₂**: Nitrogen dioxide (from vehicles and industry)
- **SO₂**: Sulfur dioxide (from coal burning)
- **CO**: Carbon monoxide (from incomplete combustion)
- **Ozone**: Ground-level ozone (from vehicle emissions + sunlight)

### 2. Water Pollution

**Sources:**

- Industrial discharge
- Sewage and domestic waste
- Agricultural runoff (pesticides, fertilizers)
- Plastic waste
- Religious practices (idols, offerings)

### 3. Noise Pollution

**Sources:**

- Traffic noise
- Construction activities
- Industrial operations
- Loudspeakers and celebrations
- Aircraft noise

### 4. Soil Pollution

**Sources:**

- Industrial waste
- Pesticides and fertilizers
- Plastic contamination
- E-waste dumping

## Health Effects of Air Pollution

**Short-term Effects:**

- Eye, nose, and throat irritation
- Headaches and dizziness
- Aggravation of asthma
- Respiratory infections
- Fatigue

**Long-term Effects:**

- Chronic respiratory diseases (asthma, COPD)
- Heart disease and stroke
- Lung cancer
- Reduced lung function in children
- Premature death

**Vulnerable Groups:**

- Children and infants
- Elderly persons
- Pregnant women
- People with existing respiratory or heart conditions

## Air Quality Index (AQI)

Understanding AQI readings:

- **0-50 (Good)**: Air quality is satisfactory
- **51-100 (Satisfactory)**: Minor breathing discomfort for sensitive people
- **101-200 (Moderate)**: Breathing discomfort for people with lung, heart disease
- **201-300 (Poor)**: Breathing discomfort for most people
- **301-400 (Very Poor)**: Respiratory illness on prolonged exposure
- **401-500 (Severe)**: Affects healthy people, seriously impacts those with existing diseases

**Check AQI:**

- CPCB's official app: "AirQ"
- Website: www.cpcb.nic.in
- Other apps: Air Visual, Sameer app

## Personal Actions to Reduce Air Pollution

### Transportation Choices

**Public Transport:**

- Metro systems reduce per-passenger emissions by 75%
- Buses reduce congestion and pollution
- Use bus or metro whenever possible

**Carpooling:**

- Share rides to work or school
- Reduce vehicles on road by 50-75%
- Use apps: Quick Ride, BlaBlaCar, sRide

**Electric Vehicles:**

- Zero tailpipe emissions
- Lower operating costs (₹0.80/km vs ₹4/km for petrol)
- Government incentives available
- Consider for next vehicle purchase

**Cycling and Walking:**

- Zero emissions
- Health benefits
- Use for short distances (< 5 km)
- Growing cycling infrastructure in cities

**Vehicle Maintenance:**

- Regular PUC (Pollution Under Control) checks
- Maintain proper tire pressure (improves efficiency by 3%)
- Service vehicles on schedule
- Don't remove catalytic converters
- Avoid unnecessary idling

### At Home

**Energy Conservation:**

- Every unit of electricity saved prevents 0.82 kg of CO₂
- Use LED bulbs and energy-efficient appliances
- Install solar panels if possible
- Reduce AC use (each degree lower increases emissions)

**Avoid Burning:**

- Never burn leaves or waste
- No firecrackers during festivals
- Use electric diyas instead of burning candles
- Avoid burning mosquito coils (use electric alternatives)

**Plant Trees:**

- One mature tree absorbs 22 kg of CO₂ per year
- Plant native species
- Join community plantation drives
- Maintain potted plants on balconies

**Reduce Dust:**

- Keep surroundings clean
- Water plants to settle dust
- Avoid sweeping dry surfaces (causes resuspension)

### Consumer Choices

**Reduce Plastic:**

- Plastic production and incineration causes air pollution
- Use cloth bags
- Avoid single-use plastics
- Choose products with minimal packaging

**Buy Local:**

- Reduces transportation emissions
- Support local economy
- Fresher products

**Eco-Friendly Products:**

- Choose products with Ecomark label
- Avoid products with harmful chemicals
- Use natural cleaning products

## Seasonal Pollution Control

### Winter (Oct-Feb) - High Pollution Season

**Why Worse in Winter:**

- Lower temperatures trap pollutants
- Crop burning in northern states
- Increased biomass burning for heating
- Diwali firecrackers
- Reduced wind speeds

**Actions:**

- Avoid morning outdoor exercise (6-10 AM)
- Use air purifiers at home
- Wear N95/N99 masks outdoors
- Keep windows closed during high AQI
- Use public transport more

### Summer (Mar-Jun) - Ozone Season

**Why Different:**

- High temperatures create ground-level ozone
- Dust storms
- Increased AC use

**Actions:**

- Avoid outdoor activities during peak heat (12-4 PM)
- Reduce electricity consumption
- Water roads and open areas to reduce dust
- Avoid using lawn equipment during high ozone days

### Monsoon (Jul-Sep) - Better Air Quality

**Why Better:**

- Rain washes away pollutants
- Reduced dust
- Cooler temperatures

**Actions:**

- Continue good practices
- Prevent waterlogging (mosquito breeding)
- Ensure proper waste disposal

## Indoor Air Quality

### Common Indoor Pollutants

- Cooking smoke and fumes
- Incense and dhoop
- Room fresheners and sprays
- Paints and varnishes
- Cleaning chemicals
- Mold and dampness
- Tobacco smoke

### Improve Indoor Air Quality

**Ventilation:**

- Open windows when outdoor AQI < 100
- Use exhaust fans in kitchen and bathrooms
- Create cross-ventilation
- Avoid sealing rooms completely

**Air Purifiers:**

- Choose HEPA filters (removes 99.97% of PM2.5)
- Size according to room size
- Maintain and replace filters regularly
- Place strategically near pollution sources

**Natural Air Purifiers (Plants):**

- **Snake Plant** (Sansevieria): Removes formaldehyde
- **Money Plant** (Pothos): Removes CO and benzene
- **Areca Palm**: Removes CO₂, produces oxygen
- **Spider Plant**: Removes formaldehyde and xylene
- **Aloe Vera**: Removes formaldehyde and benzene
- **Bamboo Palm**: Removes benzene and trichloroethylene

**Note:** 15-20 plants per 1800 sq ft can significantly improve air quality

**Cooking:**

- Use exhaust fans or chimneys (mandatory)
- Choose electric or induction over gas (cleaner)
- Pressure cook to reduce cooking time
- Avoid overheating oil
- Keep kitchen well-ventilated

**Reduce Chemical Use:**

- Use natural cleaning products (vinegar, baking soda)
- Avoid aerosol sprays
- Choose low-VOC paints
- Air out new furniture and carpets

## Water Pollution Control

### At Home

**Reduce Water Pollution:**

- Don't pour chemicals down drains
- Use eco-friendly detergents
- Fix leaks promptly
- Install water-saving fixtures
- Harvest rainwater

**Responsible Disposal:**

- Never throw medicines in toilet
- Dispose of paint, oil properly
- Don't wash vehicles near drains
- Use biodegradable products

### Water Conservation

- Saving water reduces energy used for pumping and treatment
- Fix dripping taps (saves 15,000 L/year)
- Use bucket instead of shower (saves 50 L/shower)
- Reuse RO reject water for cleaning
- Install dual-flush toilets

### Community Water Bodies

- Don't litter in rivers, lakes, ponds
- Participate in cleanup drives
- Don't immerse idols in water bodies (use artificial ponds)
- Report industrial discharge

## Noise Pollution Control

### Personal Actions

**At Home:**

- Keep TV/music at moderate levels
- Avoid loud celebrations late at night
- Use carpets and curtains to absorb sound
- Maintain household equipment (noisy ACs, mixers)

**In Public:**

- Avoid honking unnecessarily
- Respect noise restrictions (10 PM - 6 AM)
- Don't use loudspeakers without permission
- Be mindful in residential areas

### Know Your Rights

**Noise Limits (in dB):**

- Residential areas: Day 55, Night 45
- Commercial areas: Day 65, Night 55
- Industrial areas: Day 75, Night 70
- Silence zones (100m around hospitals, schools): Day 50, Night 40

**Report Violations:**

- Local police station
- State Pollution Control Board
- Use "Sameer" app

## Government Initiatives

### National Clean Air Programme (NCAP)

- Target: 20-30% reduction in PM2.5 and PM10 by 2024
- 131 cities covered
- City-specific action plans
- Continuous monitoring

### Graded Response Action Plan (GRAP)

Implemented in NCR based on AQI levels:

- Stage I (Poor): Public advisories
- Stage II (Very Poor): Restrictions on diesel generators
- Stage III (Severe): Ban on construction
- Stage IV (Severe+): Truck entry ban, school closures

### BS-VI Emission Standards

- Equivalent to Euro-VI standards
- 80% reduction in NOx from diesel vehicles
- Mandatory since April 2020

### Electric Vehicle Policy

- FAME-II subsidy scheme
- EV charging infrastructure
- State-specific incentives
- Target: 30% EV by 2030

### Renewable Energy Targets

- 500 GW by 2030
- Rooftop solar promotion
- Wind energy expansion
- Green hydrogen mission

## Emergency Measures (High AQI Days)

### When AQI > 300 (Very Poor/Severe)

**Immediate Actions:**

- Stay indoors as much as possible
- Keep windows and doors closed
- Use air purifiers
- Avoid strenuous outdoor activities
- Wear N95/N99 masks if must go out

**For Vulnerable Groups:**

- Consult doctor about medications
- Keep inhaler/emergency medicines ready
- Monitor symptoms closely
- Consider temporary relocation if possible

**School and Work:**

- Work from home if possible
- Keep children indoors
- Cancel outdoor sports and activities
- Use online classes option
