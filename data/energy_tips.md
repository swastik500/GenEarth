# Energy Saving Tips for Indian Households

## Why Energy Conservation Matters

India's energy demand is growing rapidly, and households account for **24% of total electricity consumption**. By saving energy, you can:

- Reduce electricity bills by 20-30%
- Lower carbon footprint
- Reduce dependence on fossil fuels
- Support national energy security
- Contribute to climate action

## Lighting Solutions

### LED Bulbs: The Smart Choice

**Benefits of LED bulbs:**

- Use **75% less energy** than incandescent bulbs
- Last **25 times longer**
- Available in various wattages and colors
- Eligible for government subsidies

**Cost Savings Example:**

- Traditional 60W bulb costs ₹180/year to run
- 9W LED costs ₹27/year to run
- **Savings: ₹153 per bulb per year**

**Action Steps:**

1. Replace all bulbs with LED
2. Choose warm white (2700K) for living areas
3. Use daylight (5000K) for work areas
4. Install dimmers for mood lighting

### Natural Lighting

- Open curtains during the day
- Paint walls in light colors to reflect light
- Use mirrors strategically
- Install skylights if possible
- Keep windows clean for maximum light

### Smart Lighting Habits

- Turn off lights when leaving a room
- Use task lighting instead of overhead lights
- Install motion sensors for outdoor lights
- Use timers for porch and garden lights

## Cooling and Heating

### Air Conditioning Efficiency

**Optimal AC Settings:**

- Set temperature to **24-26°C** (not below)
- Each degree lower increases power consumption by 6%
- Use ceiling fans along with AC
- Keep AC on "auto" mode, not "on"

**Maintenance Tips:**

- Clean or replace filters every month
- Service AC annually
- Check for refrigerant leaks
- Seal gaps around windows and doors

**Energy-Saving Strategies:**

- Close curtains during the hottest part of the day
- Avoid placing heat sources near the thermostat
- Use programmable thermostats
- Turn off AC 30 minutes before leaving

**Right-Sizing Your AC:**

- 1 ton AC for 100-120 sq ft
- 1.5 ton AC for 150-180 sq ft
- 2 ton AC for 200-250 sq ft
- Oversized ACs waste energy

### Ceiling Fans

- Use fans to feel 4°C cooler
- Clean fan blades regularly (dust reduces efficiency)
- Choose BEE 5-star rated fans
- Install fans at 8-9 feet height
- Use fans in counter-clockwise direction in summer

### Natural Cooling

- Plant trees on south and west sides
- Use bamboo blinds or curtains
- Apply reflective coating on roof
- Ensure cross-ventilation
- Use terrace gardens for insulation

## Kitchen Appliances

### Refrigerator Efficiency

**Best Practices:**

- Maintain temperature at 3-5°C (fridge) and -18°C (freezer)
- Don't overload or underload
- Allow hot food to cool before storing
- Defrost regularly (manual defrost models)
- Keep away from heat sources and walls

**Energy Savings:**

- Choose BEE 5-star rated models
- 5-star fridge uses 50% less energy than 1-star
- Invest in inverter technology refrigerators

### Cooking Appliances

**Pressure Cookers:**

- Use 50-75% less energy than regular cooking
- Cooks food 3-10 times faster
- Essential for energy-efficient Indian cooking

**Induction Cooktops:**

- 85-90% energy efficient (vs 40% for gas)
- Faster cooking time
- Safer and cleaner
- Government subsidies available in some states

**Microwave Ovens:**

- Use 50% less energy than conventional ovens
- Best for reheating and defrosting
- Not ideal for all Indian cooking

**Energy-Saving Cooking Tips:**

- Cover pots while cooking
- Match pot size to burner size
- Use pressure cookers for dal, rice, meat
- Soak dal and rice before cooking
- Turn off heat before food is fully cooked (residual heat finishes cooking)

## Water Heating

### Solar Water Heaters

**Benefits:**

- Save up to **1500 kWh per year**
- Payback period: 3-4 years
- Government subsidies available (up to 30%)
- No operating costs
- 20+ year lifespan

**Types:**

- Flat plate collectors (for warm climates)
- Evacuated tube collectors (for all climates)
- Sizes: 100-300 liters based on family size

### Electric Geyser Efficiency

- Set thermostat to 55-60°C (not higher)
- Turn off when not in use
- Use timer switches
- Install close to point of use
- Insulate pipes to reduce heat loss
- Choose BEE 5-star rated models

### Instant Geysers

- More efficient than storage geysers for small needs
- Use only for 1-2 taps
- No standby energy loss

## Home Electronics

### Television

- LED/LCD TVs use 60% less energy than older TVs
- Turn off when not watching (not on standby)
- Enable power-saving mode
- Adjust brightness to comfortable levels
- Unplug when going on vacation

### Computers and Laptops

- Laptops use 75% less energy than desktops
- Enable sleep mode after 15 minutes
- Turn off monitor when not in use
- Unplug chargers when fully charged
- Use power strips to cut phantom load

### Set-Top Boxes and Wi-Fi Routers

- Turn off when not in use
- Use smart plugs with timers
- These devices consume 24/7 if left on

## Washing and Cleaning

### Washing Machines

**Energy-Saving Tips:**

- Wash full loads only
- Use cold water (90% energy is for heating water)
- Front-load machines use 50% less water and energy
- Choose high spin speed to reduce drying time
- Clean lint filters regularly

### Dishwashers (if applicable)

- Run only when fully loaded
- Use eco mode
- Air-dry dishes instead of heat-dry
- Scrape dishes, don't pre-rinse

## Home Insulation and Weatherization

### Windows and Doors

- Install weather stripping
- Use double-glazed windows
- Install curtains with thermal lining
- Seal gaps and cracks

### Roofing and Walls

- Apply reflective white paint or coating
- Add insulation in attics
- Use heat-resistant tiles
- Install green/terrace gardens

## Behavioral Changes

### Daily Habits

- Unplug devices when not in use (phantom load = 5-10% of bill)
- Use natural ventilation before turning on AC
- Iron clothes in batches
- Dry clothes in sunlight, not dryer
- Avoid using multiple appliances simultaneously (reduces peak load)

### Seasonal Adjustments

**Summer:**

- Use AC only when temperature > 30°C
- Close curtains during the hottest part of day
- Use fans more, AC less

**Monsoon:**

- Reduce use of electric dryers
- Be cautious with electrical safety
- Use dehumidifiers only when necessary

**Winter:**

- Use sunlight for warmth during day
- Wear warm clothes instead of using heaters
- Use hot water bottles instead of electric blankets

## Government Schemes and Incentives

### PM Surya Ghar Yojana

- Up to 40% subsidy on rooftop solar
- Available for systems up to 3 kW
- Apply through state electricity distribution companies

### UJALA Scheme (LED bulbs)

- Subsidized LED bulbs at ₹70-80
- Bulk procurement for lower prices
- Available through state distribution companies

### Energy Efficient Appliance Programs

- Partial rebates on 5-star appliances
- Check with state electricity boards
- Special programs during festivals

### Net Metering

- Sell excess solar power back to grid
- Credits adjusted against bill
- 1:1 energy credit in most states

## Star Rating Guide (BEE)

When buying appliances, look for **Bureau of Energy Efficiency (BEE)** star ratings:

- **5 stars**: Most efficient (best choice)
- **4 stars**: Very efficient
- **3 stars**: Average efficiency
- **2 stars**: Below average
- **1 star**: Least efficient (avoid)

**Savings Example:**

- 5-star 1.5-ton AC: 1,200 kWh/year (₹7,200)
- 3-star 1.5-ton AC: 1,500 kWh/year (₹9,000)
- **Annual savings: ₹1,800**

## Calculate Your Savings

**Monthly Electricity Bill Reduction Plan:**

1. Replace 10 bulbs with LED: Save ₹100-150/month
2. Set AC to 25°C instead of 20°C: Save ₹300-400/month
3. Turn off phantom loads: Save ₹100-150/month
4. Use ceiling fans with AC: Save ₹200-250/month
5. Optimize refrigerator use: Save ₹100/month

**Total potential savings: ₹800-1,050 per month = ₹10,000-12,000 per year**

2. Identify high-consumption appliances
3. Check for air leaks and insulation
4. Assess lighting efficiency
5. Evaluate HVAC performance

- Available through BEE-certified auditors

- Can identify 20-30% savings potential
